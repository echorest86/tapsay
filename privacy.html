Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Tap & Say does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

The boards, tiles, photos, saved phrases and Recent list you create are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

No account, no internet

The app has no sign-in and makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Speech

Text is spoken by Apple's on-device voices, including Personal Voice if you choose to use it. Speech is generated on your device.

Photos

If you add a photo to a tile, you pick it yourself with the system photo picker. The app only receives the photo you choose and keeps a small copy on your device.

Face ID

If you turn on the edit lock, Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. The app never sees your face data or passcode.

Widget, Siri and Shortcuts

The widget shows fixed buttons and reads no data. When you use it, or ask Siri to say a phrase, iOS opens the app on your device to speak it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored. You can also clear Recent inside the app at any time.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a support request.
