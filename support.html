Common questions

Why can't I hear anything?

Turn up the volume. Tap & Say speaks even when the silent switch is on. If it's still quiet, open Settings in the app and tap Test voice, or pick a different voice.

Can I use my own voice?

Yes. If you've made a Personal Voice (iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Personal Voice), open Tap & Say → Settings → Use my Personal Voice, then choose it as the voice.

How do I change a tile or add a photo?

Hold any tile and choose Edit board, or go to Settings → Edit board. Tap a tile to change its picture, words, spoken text and colour, or tap + Add in an empty space.

How do I stop someone else changing the board?

Settings → Lock editing with Face ID. Editing then needs Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode.

My hands shake. What helps?

In Settings, turn on Ignore accidental double taps, try Hold to tap, choose Large tiles, and set the Type tab to start with Word tiles instead of the keyboard.

How do I add the Yes · No · Wait · Help widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Tap & Say and add it. You can also add it to your Lock Screen, or say "Say help with Tap & Say" to Siri.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Tap & Say has no account and no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own boards and phrases.
